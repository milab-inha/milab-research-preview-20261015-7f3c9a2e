Principal investigator

Hongki Lim

Assistant Professor
Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering · Inha University

Probabilistic generative modeling and inference.

My research concerns generative models, conditional sampling, and inverse problems. I am interested in the principles that connect learned distributions with observations, constraints, and rewards.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan in 2020, advised by Jeffrey Fessler and Yuni Dewaraja. Before joining Inha, I worked at Siemens Healthineers as a Senior AI Scientist.
