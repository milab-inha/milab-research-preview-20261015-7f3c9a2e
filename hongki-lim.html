Research

I develop generative models and inference methods that combine learned distributions with observations, constraints, and objectives. My goal is to make conditional generation both statistically grounded and computationally practical.

My work connects model-based optimization and learned priors, from iterative neural reconstruction to diffusion posterior sampling. Current interests include inverse imaging, 3D reconstruction, and generating diverse solutions under constraints.
